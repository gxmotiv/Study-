Lesson 8 · Measures of Central Tendency

A measure of central tendency gives a representative value for a whole data set.

Four measures in your notes: Arithmetic Mean, Median, Quartiles and Mode.

Arithmetic Mean: Add all values and divide by the number of values.

X̄ = ΣX / N

Grouped data, direct method: X̄ = ΣfX / Σf
